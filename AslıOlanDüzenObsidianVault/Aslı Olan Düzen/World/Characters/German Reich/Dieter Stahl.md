# Dieter Stahl

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Oberbefehlshaber der Luftwaffe  
**Common Name:** Luftmarschall Dieter Stahl  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Der Überschallmann  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Luftwaffe High Command]]  
**Position:** Commander of the Luftwaffe  
**Rank:** Luftmarschall  
**Current Assignment:** Strategic aviation, Horten fleet, Me 620 prestige program  
**Primary Location:** Luftwaffenkommando, Germania / Rechlin test facilities  
**Date of Birth:** 1935  
**Age:** 50 in 1985  
**Origin:** Stuttgart

## Role
Commands air force modernization and strategic aviation competition with Turkey.

## Formal Authority
Controls Luftwaffe formations, air doctrine, air bases and aviation testing.

## Practical Influence
Can influence German prestige through spectacular aircraft programs.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** air deployments, demonstrations, test programs
- **Cannot order:** industrial quotas or diplomatic messaging alone
- **Can be ignored by:** armaments ministry can fail delivery; SS can hide intelligence
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] via [[Karl-Heinz Adler]]
- **Protected by:** aviation prestige and propaganda usefulness
- **Limited by:** fuel, engines, radar competition, Turkish advances

## Reports To
[[Karl-Heinz Adler]]

## Direct Subordinates
- Strategic aviation command
- Rechlin test staff

## Political Relationships
- Works with [[Viktor Hanisch]] but blames industry for delays.
- Used by [[Gerda Weiss]] for propaganda imagery.

## Military Relationships
- Rivalry with [[Klaus Brenner]] over strategic budget.

## Personality
Brilliant, vain, technically literate.

## Leadership Style
Rewards pilots who produce spectacle and data.

## Ideology / Beliefs
German skies must remain psychologically dominant.

## Personal Motivation
Prove Horten-derived systems still outclass Turkish Gökkanat designs.

## Strengths
Technical ambition.

## Weaknesses
Underestimates Turkish independent innovation.

## Fears
A Turkish sensor breakthrough neutralizing stealth aircraft.

## Ambitions
Build the next generation of invisible strategic aircraft.

## History
Rose through test aviation and supersonic prestige programs.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Indirectly tied to Istanbul Horten/Gökkanat display seen by [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]].

## Current Situation
Defending the Istanbul demonstration as successful despite Turkish response.

## Potential Future Development
