## Role
Leads diplomacy, embassies, treaty language, and official communications with Germany and other powers.

## Formal Authority
Commands diplomatic missions and treaty negotiation teams.

## Practical Influence
Can buy time or provoke crisis through phrasing.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** ambassadorial instructions, note drafts, diplomatic protests
- **Cannot order:** covert operations, troop deployments, propaganda without coordination
- **Can be ignored by:** intelligence may run channels she is not told about
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** international expertise and Selim’s trust
- **Limited by:** military facts and ideological expectations

## Leadership Style

## Ideology / Beliefs

## Strengths

## Weaknesses
