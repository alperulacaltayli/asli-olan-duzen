# Haluk Sancar

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Afrika Umumi Valisi  
**Common Name:** Haluk Sancar  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Güney Valisi  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Imperial Regional Administration]]  
**Position:** Governor-general for African imperial territories  
**Rank:** Umumi Vali  
**Current Assignment:** Implementing post-slavery administrative reform in African territories  
**Primary Location:** Kahire-Güney İdare Merkezi / rotating field offices  
**Date of Birth:** 1927  
**Age:** 58 in 1985  
**Origin:** Mardin

## Role
Civil-military administrator for African regions under Turkish imperial authority.

## Formal Authority
Coordinates regional governors, infrastructure, security and transition away from former German-style forced labor systems.

## Practical Influence
Controls what “reform” means on the ground.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** regional administrative changes, local security coordination, labor transition plans
- **Cannot order:** imperial foreign policy or army-wide deployment
- **Can be ignored by:** local commanders, industrial contractors, and tribal/local authorities can obstruct
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]] through interior/regional review
- **Protected by:** distance and specialization
- **Limited by:** logistics, corruption, language barriers, old abuses

## Reports To
[[Alp Erden]] administratively and [[Selim Arkan]] on special reports

## Direct Subordinates
- African provincial governors
- Reform inspection teams

## Political Relationships
- Worries Ankara wants clean propaganda more than messy truth.

## Military Relationships
- Depends on regional military commands for security.

## Personality
Tired, pragmatic, not cruel but not naïve.

## Leadership Style
Trades ideals for workable steps, one district at a time.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Empire must prove it is better than German brutality by actual conditions, not slogans.

## Personal Motivation
Prevent Africa reform from becoming another imperial stain.

## Strengths
Ground realism.

## Weaknesses
Can tolerate too much compromise.

## Fears
Exposure of local abuses under Turkish authority.

## Ambitions
Create a model province that embarrasses German rule by comparison.

## History
Former provincial governor sent south because he solved famine logistics.

## Current Situation
Implementing reforms shown in the Chapter 1 news broadcast.
