**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Oberkommando der Wehrmacht]]  
**Position:** Chief of Armed Forces High Command  
**Rank:** Generalfeldmarschall

**Primary Location:** Wehrmachtführungsstab, Germania

## Role
Senior professional soldier coordinating army, air force and navy.

## Formal Authority
Commands high military planning and operational coordination under the Führer.

## Practical Influence
Can shape what military risks seem acceptable.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** staff planning, readiness reviews, inter-branch exercises
- **Cannot order:** declare war, overrule SS security zones, set ideology
- **Can be ignored by:** SS commanders and party governors in occupied territories
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]]
- **Protected by:** army respect and military necessity
- **Limited by:** Führer directives, SS interference, industrial shortages

## Personality
Professional, aristocratic, exhausted by politics.

## Leadership Style
Staff-driven, formal, precise.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Nationalist and authoritarian; less mystical than SS.

## Personal Motivation
Keep the armed forces from becoming party theater.

## Strengths
Strategic realism.

## Weaknesses
Too cautious for party radicals, too political for pure soldiers.

## Fears
A two-front strategic overstretch against Turkey and colonial unrest.

## Ambitions
Restore military autonomy.

## History
