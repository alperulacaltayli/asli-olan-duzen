## Role
Protects sea routes, straits, naval aviation, and imperial maritime supply.

## Formal Authority
Commands fleets, naval bases, maritime patrol and naval logistics.

## Practical Influence
Controls the empire’s ability to move resources between continents.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** fleet readiness, patrol schedules, naval base security
- **Cannot order:** foreign policy, army deployments, or air force procurement
- **Can be ignored by:** land high command during continental crises; finance can slow shipbuilding
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]] via [[Turan Erbay]]
- **Protected by:** control of straits expertise
- **Limited by:** shipyard capacity and fuel allocation

Patient, maritime, quietly sarcastic.

## Leadership Style
