# Helmut Drexler

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Direktor des Reichsforschungsamtes für Sondertechnik  
**Common Name:** Dr. Helmut Drexler  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Reich Scientific and Special Technology Office]]  
**Position:** Director of special technology research  
**Rank:** Scientific director  
**Current Assignment:** Advanced aviation, sensors, rocketry, exotic materials  
**Primary Location:** Peenemünde-Rechlin Research Corridor  
**Date of Birth:** 1940  
**Age:** 45 in 1985  
**Origin:** Jena

## Role
Coordinates high-risk scientific programs under armaments and SS pressure.

## Formal Authority
Controls research proposals and classified laboratories for special technology.

## Practical Influence
Can bury inconvenient results or exaggerate promising ones.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** lab priorities, classified tests, scientist transfers
- **Cannot order:** mass production or military adoption alone
- **Can be ignored by:** armaments minister can override; SS can seize projects
- **Can be removed by:** [[Viktor Hanisch]] or SS intervention
- **Protected by:** technical indispensability
- **Limited by:** physics and political fantasy

## Reports To
[[Viktor Hanisch]] with SS oversight pressure

## Direct Subordinates
- Sensor lab chiefs
- Aerodynamics institutes

## Political Relationships
- Squeezed between [[Viktor Hanisch]] and [[Friedrich Wulfhart]].

## Military Relationships
- Provides data to [[Dieter Stahl]].

## Personality
Nervous genius, ethically compromised.

## Leadership Style
Protects good scientists by feeding superiors partial optimism.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Believes German science must lead, but privately fears ideology is corrupting science.

## Personal Motivation
Keep laboratories alive without being swallowed by SS.

## Strengths
Technical breadth.

## Weaknesses
Cowardice under political pressure.

## Fears
Turkish scientists solving practical problems faster than German prestige labs.

## Ambitions
Build a sensor countermeasure program.

## History
Postwar child prodigy absorbed into state research.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Could become German scientific counterpart to [[Rana Tözün]].

## Current Situation
Awaiting technical results from Istanbul aviation observation.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Might identify Oğuz-style LIDAR detection as a strategic threat.
