# Ulrich von Havel

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Reichsaußenminister  
**Common Name:** Ulrich von Havel  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Reich Foreign Office]]  
**Position:** Foreign minister  
**Rank:** Minister  
**Current Assignment:** Managing the public fiction of Turkish-German friendship  
**Primary Location:** Auswärtiges Amt, Germania  
**Date of Birth:** 1932  
**Age:** 53 in 1985  
**Origin:** Potsdam

## Role
Runs German diplomacy and official relations with Turkey and other powers.

## Formal Authority
Controls embassies, diplomatic notes and treaty channels.

## Practical Influence
Often cleans up crises caused by military and SS operations.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** ambassadors, diplomatic cover stories, formal protests
- **Cannot order:** SS operations or air demonstrations once approved above him
- **Can be ignored by:** SS and military covert channels
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]]
- **Protected by:** elite background and foreign contacts
- **Limited by:** ideological line and security interference

## Reports To
[[Reinhard Falkenhorst]]

## Direct Subordinates
- German mission to Istanbul/Ankara
- Treaty departments

## Political Relationships
- Frustrated by [[Friedrich Wulfhart]]’s covert provocations.
- Needs [[Gerda Weiss]] to keep public line coherent.

## Military Relationships
- Has little control over demonstrations planned by [[Dieter Stahl]].

## Personality
Elegant, cynical, tired.

## Leadership Style
Diplomacy as damage control.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Believes Reich superiority but knows arrogance can be expensive.

## Personal Motivation
Keep Turkey close enough to study and far enough not to dominate.

## Strengths
Reading diplomatic signals.

## Weaknesses
Weak against SS secrecy.

## Fears
An SS operation destroying years of diplomatic positioning.

## Ambitions
Engineer a treaty that limits Turkish strategic independence.

## History
Aristocratic diplomat who survived by serving every Führer loyally enough.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- His ministry publicly frames events seen by [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]] as friendship.

## Current Situation
Preparing statement on the Istanbul visit.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could be the face of German politeness while others prepare coercion.
