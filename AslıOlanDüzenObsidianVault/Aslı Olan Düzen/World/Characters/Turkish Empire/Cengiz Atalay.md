# Cengiz Atalay

**Status:** Established Canon / Author-confirmed Expansion  
**Official Title:** Askerî Uçak Mühendisi; Gizli Alman Kanal Görevlisi  
**Common Name:** Cengiz Bey  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None publicly  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Imperial Intelligence Directorate]]  
**Position:** Military aircraft engineer with Nazi connection and secret mission  
**Rank:** Civilian-military technical specialist  
**Current Assignment:** Maintain controlled clandestine contact with German aviation/security circles  
**Primary Location:** İstanbul military aviation facilities / Atalay family home, Üsküdar  
**Date of Birth:** 1942  
**Age:** 43 in 1985  
**Origin:** İstanbul

## Role
Publicly, Cengiz is an aircraft engineer and father of [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]]. Secretly, he maintains a Nazi-linked channel as part of a covert mission.

## Formal Authority
As engineer, controls technical work packages only within assigned projects. As clandestine asset, authority is compartmentalized and mission-specific.

## Practical Influence
Knows more about German aviation interest than he can admit. His family position makes secrecy emotionally dangerous.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** limited technical subordinates in his engineering section; no broad political commands
- **Cannot order:** military deployments, intelligence operations beyond his assigned channel, or state policy
- **Can be ignored by:** senior officers, intelligence handlers, and German contacts can all constrain him
- **Can be removed by:** his engineering command or covert handler, likely [[Mete Kutlu]] — Status: Proposed
- **Protected by:** technical value, compartmentalized mission, family-man public profile
- **Limited by:** secrecy, surveillance risk, family trust, German suspicion

## Reports To
Engineering chain publicly; covertly to a classified handler in [[Imperial Intelligence Directorate]] — Status: Proposed

## Direct Subordinates
- Junior aviation engineers — names BELİRLENMESİ GEREKİYOR

## Political Relationships
- His covert Nazi connection could become a scandal if [[Alp Erden]] discovers it outside authorized channels.

## Military Relationships
- Connected to aviation programs relevant to [[Gökhan Aydemir]] and technical bureaus.

## Personality
Warm father at home; guarded and controlled when work intrudes.

## Leadership Style
Mentors through questions; avoids giving direct answers when secrecy is involved.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Believes the empire must survive German pressure, but his methods force him to speak the enemy’s language.

## Personal Motivation
Protect his family while completing a mission that may prevent worse conflict.

## Strengths
Technical skill; emotional restraint; credibility with German contacts.

## Weaknesses
Secrecy damages trust; son is technically sharp enough to notice inconsistencies.

## Fears
[[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]] discovering the wrong half of the truth too early.

## Ambitions
Finish the clandestine channel without losing his family or betraying the empire.

## History
Worked on military aircraft development; his father contributed to early TA-85 prototypes according to Oğuz’s dialogue.
