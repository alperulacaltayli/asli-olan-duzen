# Turkish Imperial High Command Structure

**Status:** Proposed Structure built from established canon and author decisions  
**State:** [[Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu]]  
**Current Year:** 1985

## Supreme Authority
[[Selim Arkan]] — [[Ulu Hanlık Makamı]]

## High Political Authority
- [[Kaan Demirsoy]] — [[Imperial Chancellery]] / Başbuyrukçu
- [[Safiye Kutadgu]] — [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs]]
- [[Alp Erden]] — [[Ministry of Interior and Order]]
- [[Ziya Arat]] — [[Ministry of Education and Youth Order]]
- [[Cevdet Bayraktar]] — [[Ministry of Industry and Armaments]]
- [[Rana Tözün]] — [[Science and Strategic Research Council]]

## High Security Council
[[Kutlu Güvenlik Meclisi]] forces rival institutions to share assessments before [[Selim Arkan]].

## Military High Command
[[Selim Arkan]] → [[Turan Erbay]] → branch commanders:
- [[Börü Alkan]] — [[Imperial Army]]
- [[Gökhan Aydemir]] — [[Imperial Air Force]]
- [[Deniz Koray]] — [[Imperial Navy]]

## Intelligence and Security
- [[Mete Kutlu]] — [[Imperial Intelligence Directorate]]; foreign/strategic intelligence.
- [[Alp Erden]] → [[Selahattin Akgün]] — [[Internal Security Directorate]]; domestic order.

## Regional Authority
- [[Alp Erden]] supervises ordinary regional administration.
- [[Haluk Sancar]] administers African reforms as [[Afrika Umumi Valisi]].

## Aviation and Science Chain
[[Selim Arkan]] → [[Rana Tözün]] / [[Cevdet Bayraktar]] / [[Gökhan Aydemir]] → [[Baran İleri]] and technical bureaus.  
[[Cengiz Atalay]] sits at the dangerous intersection of aviation engineering and covert intelligence.

## Major Internal Rivalries
- [[Alp Erden]] vs [[Mete Kutlu]] — domestic security versus foreign intelligence boundaries.
- [[Turan Erbay]] vs [[Safiye Kutadgu]] — military readiness versus diplomatic time-buying.
- [[Gökhan Aydemir]] vs [[Deniz Koray]] / [[Börü Alkan]] — aviation prestige versus balanced force structure.
- [[Cevdet Bayraktar]] vs [[Rana Tözün]] — production realism versus scientific caution.

## Secret / Informal Structure
**Status: Proposed / Classified.** [[Cengiz Atalay]] maintains a Nazi-linked channel under a secret mission. The exact handler is proposed as [[Mete Kutlu]] but requires author approval.
