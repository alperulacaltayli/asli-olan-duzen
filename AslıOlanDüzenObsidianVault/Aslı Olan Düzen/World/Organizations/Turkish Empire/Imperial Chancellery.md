# Imperial Chancellery

**Status:** Proposed Organization  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Type:** Central administrative office  
**Headquarters:** Kutlu Saray Administrative Wing, Ankara  
**Leader:** [[Kaan Demirsoy]]  
**Second-in-command:** [[Sabiha Erten]]  
**Established:** [BELİRLENMESİ GEREKİYOR]  
**Purpose:** Turns the Ulu Han’s will into documents, procedures and enforceable decrees.

## Responsibilities
Decrees, meeting agendas, classified minutes, archival routing, inter-ministry coordination.

## Internal Structure
Decree Bureau, Classified Minutes Office, Appointment Office, Archive Control Desk.

## Chain of Command
[[Selim Arkan]] → [[Kaan Demirsoy]] → bureau chiefs.

## Major Personnel
- [[Kaan Demirsoy]]
- [[Sabiha Erten]]

## Political Influence
Controls wording and priority of state action.

## Military Influence
No direct command; routes authorized military decrees.

## Resources
Archives, seals, document circulation authority.

## Rival Institutions
- [[Ministry of Interior and Order]]

## Allies
- [[Ulu Hanlık Makamı]]
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs]]

## Current Situation
Preparing classified records after German visit.

## Historical Background
Expanded as imperial system became too large for personal rule.

## Public Structure
Publicly described as a lawful state institution serving the national order.

## Internal Structure
Internally divided into formal offices, personal networks, and senior advisers.

## Secret Structure
Status: Proposed / Classified. Secret channels may exist, but specific operations require author approval.
