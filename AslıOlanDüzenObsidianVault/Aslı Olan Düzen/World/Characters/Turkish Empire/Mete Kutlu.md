# Mete Kutlu

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** İmparatorluk İstihbarat Başkanı  
**Common Name:** Mete Kutlu  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Sessiz Börü  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Imperial Intelligence Directorate]]  
**Position:** Director of foreign and strategic intelligence  
**Rank:** Director  
**Current Assignment:** German intentions, foreign networks, strategic warning  
**Primary Location:** Gizli Karargâh, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1931  
**Age:** 54 in 1985  
**Origin:** Bursa

## Role
Runs foreign intelligence, strategic analysis, and covert channels.

## Formal Authority
Controls external intelligence stations and strategic counterintelligence outside normal police authority.

## Practical Influence
Can decide what the ruler sees first, but cannot act openly without political cover.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** surveillance abroad, source handling, analysis priorities, covert liaison
- **Cannot order:** domestic mass arrests or military deployments
- **Can be ignored by:** Interior police in purely domestic cases; military intelligence in battlefield channels
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** classified successes and secrets known about many officials
- **Limited by:** need for deniability and limited verified information

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]] and selected sessions of [[Kutlu Güvenlik Meclisi]]

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Cengiz Atalay]]
- Senior German Desk analysts

## Political Relationships
- Rivalry with [[Alp Erden]] over who owns counterintelligence inside the empire.
- Quiet dependence on [[Safiye Kutadgu]] for diplomatic cover.

## Military Relationships
- Feeds assessments to [[Turan Erbay]] but does not command troops.

## Personality
Quiet, patient, unnerving.

## Leadership Style
Compartmentalizes; subordinates know only what they must.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Survival requires secrets; morality matters only if the state survives long enough to answer for it.

## Personal Motivation
Understand Germany before Germany understands the empire.

## Strengths
Network discipline.

## Weaknesses
Can become blind to human cost.

## Fears
A double agent inside aviation programs.

## Ambitions
Build a German desk capable of predicting succession struggles in Germania.

## History
Former diplomatic cryptographer; rose by exposing a German industrial collection ring.
