# Turan Erbay

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Ordu Başbuğu  
**Common Name:** Mareşal Turan Erbay  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Demir Başbuğ  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Turkish Imperial High Command]]  
**Position:** Chief of the General Staff  
**Rank:** Mareşal  
**Current Assignment:** Overall military readiness against Germany and other rivals  
**Primary Location:** Başbuğluk Karargâhı, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1922  
**Age:** 63 in 1985  
**Origin:** Sivas

## Role
Highest professional soldier beneath [[Selim Arkan]]. Coordinates land, air, naval, strategic and regional commands.

## Formal Authority
Commands the unified high command and issues operational directives once authorized.

## Practical Influence
Can heavily influence what options the ruler sees as militarily realistic.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** readiness reviews, exercises, operational planning, inter-branch coordination
- **Cannot order:** declare war, override diplomatic instructions, or initiate strategic strike without Ulu Han approval
- **Can be ignored by:** branch commanders can contest feasibility; intelligence can withhold unverified claims
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]] with high council support
- **Protected by:** prestige, officer corps respect, war record
- **Limited by:** logistics, politics, and Selim Arkan’s final authority

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]]

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Gökhan Aydemir]]
- [[Deniz Koray]]
- [[Börü Alkan]]

## Political Relationships
- Respects [[Selim Arkan]] but worries civilian caution can waste military advantage.
- Clashes with [[Safiye Kutadgu]] over German appeasement optics.

## Military Relationships
- Direct superior to [[Gökhan Aydemir]], [[Deniz Koray]], [[Börü Alkan]].

## Personality
Hard, restrained, suspicious of rhetoric.

## Leadership Style
Demands preparedness and hates improvisation disguised as bravery.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Military strength preserves peace; weakness invites humiliation.

## Personal Motivation
Ensure the empire is never strategically surprised by Germany.

## Strengths
Operational clarity; officer loyalty.

## Weaknesses
Limited patience for diplomacy and public opinion.

## Fears
German first strike or internal complacency.

## Ambitions
Build an integrated command system independent of foreign doctrine.

## History
Veteran of border crises and early imperial consolidation campaigns.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- May one day notice [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]] only if Oğuz’s project reaches military channels.

## Current Situation
Raising readiness quietly after the Horten/Gökkanat display.
