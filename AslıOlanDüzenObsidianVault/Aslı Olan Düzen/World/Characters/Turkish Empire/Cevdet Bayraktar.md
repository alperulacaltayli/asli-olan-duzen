# Cevdet Bayraktar

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Sanayi ve Silahlanma Nazırı  
**Common Name:** Cevdet Bayraktar  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Çelik Nazır  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Ministry of Industry and Armaments]]  
**Position:** Minister of industry and armaments  
**Rank:** Senior minister  
**Current Assignment:** Aircraft production, armament quotas, strategic industry  
**Primary Location:** Sanayi Nezareti, Ankara / Eskişehir industrial corridor  
**Date of Birth:** 1929  
**Age:** 56 in 1985  
**Origin:** Kocaeli

## Role
Controls factories, procurement schedules, armaments priorities, and industrial mobilization.

## Formal Authority
Directs state industry and military production contracts.

## Practical Influence
Can make or break air force modernization through bottlenecks.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** factory priorities, production audits, procurement allocation
- **Cannot order:** operational use of weapons or diplomatic concessions
- **Can be ignored by:** private industrial patrons may delay; military may reject specifications
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** industrial network and production knowledge
- **Limited by:** raw materials, skilled labor, transport

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]] and economic council

## Direct Subordinates
- Major factory directors
- Aviation design bureaus

## Political Relationships
- Budget rivalry with [[Gökhan Aydemir]] and [[Deniz Koray]].
- Suspicious of [[Mete Kutlu]] inserting secret requirements.

## Military Relationships
- Supplies [[Imperial Air Force]], [[Imperial Army]], [[Imperial Navy]].

## Personality
Pragmatic, impatient, numbers-driven.

## Leadership Style
Measures loyalty in delivered tonnage and working engines.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Industrial independence is sovereignty.

## Personal Motivation
End hidden dependence on German technical standards.

## Strengths
Production realism.

## Weaknesses
Dismisses moral/political fallout as “nonproductive.”

## Fears
German embargo on critical machine tools.

## Ambitions
Build fully domestic aircraft and sensor supply chain.

## History
Industrial planner from postwar modernization drives.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- May indirectly touch [[Cengiz Atalay]] via aviation bureaus.

## Current Situation
Reviewing whether German visit masked industrial espionage.

## Potential Future Development
