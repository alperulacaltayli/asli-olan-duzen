**Rank:** Reichsführer-SS

**Primary Location:** SS-Hauptamt Complex, Germania

**Age:** 60 in 1985

## Role
Commands SS, domestic security, and much of the Reich’s coercive state.

## Formal Authority
Controls SS formations, security police, internal counterintelligence, camps/security territories.

## Practical Influence
Can terrify ministers and generals but still needs political cover for major moves.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** security investigations, arrests under security law, SS deployments
- **Cannot order:** regular army campaigns or economic production quotas alone
- **Can be ignored by:** army field commands resist SS intrusion in war zones
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] if coalition forms; otherwise extremely difficult
- **Protected by:** SS loyalty, secret files, fear
- **Limited by:** Führer balancing, army suspicion, industrial dependence
