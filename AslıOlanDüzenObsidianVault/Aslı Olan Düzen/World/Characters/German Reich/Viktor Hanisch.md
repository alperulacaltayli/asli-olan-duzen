# Viktor Hanisch

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Reichsminister für Rüstung und Sonderwaffen  
**Common Name:** Viktor Hanisch  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Reich Armaments and Special Weapons Ministry]]  
**Position:** Armaments and special weapons minister  
**Rank:** Minister  
**Current Assignment:** Weapons production, special aviation, strategic programs  
**Primary Location:** Rüstungsministerium, Germania / Peenemünde-Rechlin corridor  
**Date of Birth:** 1933  
**Age:** 52 in 1985  
**Origin:** Essen

## Role
Turns industrial capacity into weapons, prioritizing special projects.

## Formal Authority
Controls armaments contracts, strategic weapons program funding, and weapons design bureaus.

## Practical Influence
Can starve conventional forces to feed prestige weapons.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** design priorities, production reallocations, weapons trials
- **Cannot order:** operational deployment without military approval
- **Can be ignored by:** economic ministry controls some raw inputs; SS may hide projects
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]]
- **Protected by:** special weapons mystique
- **Limited by:** materials, engineering reality, inter-ministry rivalry

## Reports To
[[Reinhard Falkenhorst]]

## Direct Subordinates
- Weapons bureaus
- Aviation special projects

## Political Relationships
- Bitter rivalry with [[Ernst Albrecht Krauss]].
- Deals uneasily with [[Dieter Stahl]].

## Military Relationships
- Supplies Luftwaffe and Wehrmacht; blamed for delays.

## Personality
Ambitious, secretive, addicted to breakthrough promises.

## Leadership Style
Funds competing teams and lets them fight.

## Ideology / Beliefs

## Strengths

## Weaknesses
