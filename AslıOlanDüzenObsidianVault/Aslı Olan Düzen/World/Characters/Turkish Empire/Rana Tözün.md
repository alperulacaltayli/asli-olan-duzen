# Rana Tözün

**Status:** Proposed Character

**Common Name:** Prof. Dr. Rana Tözün  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Bilge Hatun  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Science and Strategic Research Council]]  
**Position:** Head of imperial science council  
**Rank:** Senior scientific administrator  
**Current Assignment:** Space, advanced sensors, universities, weapons-adjacent science  
**Primary Location:** Ankara Bilim Yerleşkesi  
**Date of Birth:** 1938  
**Age:** 47 in 1985  
**Origin:** İstanbul

## Role
Coordinates scientific research priorities, university-state projects, and strategic technology programs.

## Formal Authority
Controls grants, classified research approvals, and scientific advisory committees.

## Practical Influence
Can legitimate or bury unusual ideas.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** research reviews, funding panels, classified feasibility studies
- **Cannot order:** factory production or combat deployments
- **Can be ignored by:** military can classify projects away from civilian science
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** space program prestige
- **Limited by:** budgets and security compartmentalization

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]] through science council sessions

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Baran İleri]]
- University liaison offices

## Political Relationships
- Must negotiate with [[Cevdet Bayraktar]] for turning research into production.
- Wary of [[Alp Erden]] policing academic debate.

## Military Relationships
- Advises [[Turkish Imperial High Command]] on strategic technology.

## Personality
Analytical, unsentimental, quietly humane.

## Leadership Style
Forces scientists to state assumptions and uncertainty.

## Ideology / Beliefs
A strong empire must know truth even when truth is inconvenient.

## Personal Motivation
Keep science from becoming mere propaganda.

## Strengths
Institutional credibility.

## Weaknesses
Limited control once projects become weapons.

## Fears
Past abuses hidden under “scientific necessity.”

## Ambitions
Build an independent research culture.

## History
Key civilian figure in atmospheric and orbital research after the moon achievement.

## Relationship to Other Characters
