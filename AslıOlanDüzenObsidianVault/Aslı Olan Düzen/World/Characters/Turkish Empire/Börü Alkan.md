# Börü Alkan

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Kara Kuvvetleri Başbuğu  
**Common Name:** Orgeneral Börü Alkan  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Sınır Kurdu  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Imperial Army]]  
**Position:** Commander of the Imperial Army  
**Rank:** Orgeneral  
**Current Assignment:** Land forces, borders, mechanized armies  
**Primary Location:** Kara Karargâhı, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1924  
**Age:** 61 in 1985  
**Origin:** Erzurum

## Role
Commands land force readiness and regional ground defense.

## Formal Authority
Controls armies, corps, military districts and ground training.

## Practical Influence
Influences internal security during crises because troops are everywhere.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** ground exercises, border reinforcement, corps inspections
- **Cannot order:** domestic arrests without security ministry coordination
- **Can be ignored by:** regional governors can complicate movement; air/naval branches have own chains
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]] via [[Turan Erbay]]
- **Protected by:** land officer network
- **Limited by:** rail logistics and political sensitivity of troop movements

## Reports To

## Direct Subordinates

- Anadolu İç Bölge Komutanlığı

## Political Relationships
- Wary of [[Alp Erden]] using soldiers for police work.

## Military Relationships
- Rival-budget relationship with [[Gökhan Aydemir]].

## Personality
Blunt, physical, traditional.

## Leadership Style
Inspects personally and distrusts polished reports.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Empire rests on disciplined land and loyal borders.

## Personal Motivation
Keep the army respected in a world fascinated by aircraft and space.

## Strengths
Border realism.

## Weaknesses
Suspicious of new technology he cannot see working.

## Fears
Internal unrest forcing army into ugly domestic role.

## Ambitions
Modern mechanized doctrine.

## History
Commanded harsh frontier stabilization operations; some old abuses may attach to his generation.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- No direct current protagonist connection.

## Current Situation
Preparing contingency plans for western frontier tension.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could embody the empire’s older, harsher military memory.
