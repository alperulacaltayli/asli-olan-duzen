**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Chef des Auslandsnachrichtendienstes  
**Common Name:** Leonhard Krüger  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Reich Foreign Intelligence Service]]  
**Position:** Foreign intelligence chief  
**Rank:** Director  
**Current Assignment:** Foreign espionage, Turkey desk, technical collection  
**Primary Location:** Nachrichtendienst Zentrale, Germania  
**Date of Birth:** 1937  
**Age:** 48 in 1985  
**Origin:** Hannover

## Role
Runs foreign intelligence separate from, but pressured by, SS security.

## Formal Authority
Controls foreign agents, diplomatic cover intelligence and strategic analysis.

## Practical Influence
Can collect what diplomats deny and soldiers demand.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** foreign source operations, technical intelligence collection
- **Cannot order:** domestic arrests or SS internal purges
- **Can be ignored by:** SS may run parallel channels; diplomats may deny cover
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] or absorbed by [[Friedrich Wulfhart]]
- **Protected by:** information value and foreign networks
- **Limited by:** SS rivalry and source reliability

## Reports To
[[Reinhard Falkenhorst]], with SS oversight pressure

## Direct Subordinates
- Turkey Desk
- Industrial espionage cells

## Political Relationships
- Constant pressure from [[Friedrich Wulfhart]].
- Cooperates with [[Ulrich von Havel]] when cover is needed.

## Military Relationships
- Feeds aviation intelligence to [[Dieter Stahl]] and [[Viktor Hanisch]].

## Personality
Cautious, patient, morally hollow but professionally serious.

## Leadership Style
Compartmentalizes and distrusts ideological amateurs.

## Ideology / Beliefs
The Reich needs truth from abroad even when truth humiliates ideology.

## Personal Motivation
Prove his service is more useful than SS terror abroad.

## Strengths
Analytical honesty.

## Weaknesses
Politically vulnerable to SS accusations.

## Fears
A false report pleasing leadership but causing disaster.

## Ambitions
Preserve independent foreign intelligence.

## History
Built career from signals and industrial espionage.
