# German Reich High Command Structure

**Status:** Proposed Structure / Requires Author Approval  
**State:** [[German Reich]]  
**Current Year:** 1985

## Supreme Political Leadership
[[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] — [[Reich Chancellery]], Führer und Reichskanzler.

## Party Authority
[[Martin Kessler]] — [[NSDAP Party Chancellery]], deputy and party machine controller.

## SS / Security Authority
[[Friedrich Wulfhart]] — [[SS-Reichssicherheitsapparat]].  
Subordinate: [[Hans-Georg Mertens]] — Gestapo/domestic security.

## Military High Command
[[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] → [[Karl-Heinz Adler]] — [[Oberkommando der Wehrmacht]] → branch commanders:
- [[Otto Reimann]] — [[Heer High Command]]
- [[Dieter Stahl]] — [[Luftwaffe High Command]]
- [[Klaus Brenner]] — [[Kriegsmarine High Command]]

## Ministries
- [[Ulrich von Havel]] — [[Reich Foreign Office]]
- [[Gerda Weiss]] — [[Reich Propaganda Ministry]]
- [[Ernst Albrecht Krauss]] — [[Reich Economic Ministry]]
- [[Viktor Hanisch]] — [[Reich Armaments and Special Weapons Ministry]]

## Intelligence
- [[Leonhard Krüger]] — [[Reich Foreign Intelligence Service]]; foreign espionage.
- [[Friedrich Wulfhart]] / [[Hans-Georg Mertens]] — SS/Gestapo domestic and ideological security.

## Occupied / Colonial Authority
[[Bruno Epp]] — [[Reich Colonial Administration]], especially African forced-labor remnants and resource extraction.

## Science / Technology
[[Helmut Drexler]] — [[Reich Scientific and Special Technology Office]], technically under [[Viktor Hanisch]] but pressured by SS and Luftwaffe.

## Logistics
[[Anneliese Roth]] — [[Reich Transport and Logistics Authority]], formally economic/logistics, practically able to see mobilization patterns.

## Major Internal Rivalries
- [[Friedrich Wulfhart]] vs [[Karl-Heinz Adler]] — SS security versus professional military autonomy.
- [[Martin Kessler]] vs [[Friedrich Wulfhart]] — party cadre control versus SS coercive power.
- [[Dieter Stahl]] vs [[Viktor Hanisch]] — air force demands versus armaments promises.
- [[Ernst Albrecht Krauss]] vs [[Viktor Hanisch]] — economic allocation versus special weapons obsession.
- [[Leonhard Krüger]] vs SS apparatus — foreign truth versus ideological security.
