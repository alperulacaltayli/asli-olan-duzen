# Otto Reimann

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Oberbefehlshaber des Heeres  
**Common Name:** Generaloberst Otto Reimann  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Heer High Command]]  
**Position:** Commander of the Army  
**Rank:** Generaloberst  
**Current Assignment:** Continental armies, armored doctrine, occupation garrisons  
**Primary Location:** Heereskommando, Germania  
**Date of Birth:** 1927  
**Age:** 58 in 1985  
**Origin:** Breslau

## Role
Commands German land forces and continental occupation-ready formations.

## Formal Authority
Controls army groups, armored forces, and land training.

## Practical Influence
Influences war feasibility more than public propaganda admits.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** army readiness, corps transfers, doctrine trials
- **Cannot order:** SS police actions or colonial civil administration
- **Can be ignored by:** SS in security zones; party governors in political matters
- **Can be removed by:** [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]] via [[Karl-Heinz Adler]]
- **Protected by:** army officer corps
- **Limited by:** fuel, rail logistics, political distrust

## Reports To
[[Karl-Heinz Adler]]

## Direct Subordinates
- Army group commanders

## Political Relationships
- Dislikes SS encroachment under [[Friedrich Wulfhart]].

## Military Relationships
- Competes with Luftwaffe for modernization funding.

## Personality
Gruff, practical, not ideological enough for party radicals.

## Leadership Style
Field inspections and brutal honesty.

## Ideology / Beliefs
German military supremacy, but prioritizes capability over racial theater.

## Personal Motivation
Avoid an unwinnable prestige war.

## Strengths
Operational realism.

## Weaknesses
Poor political instincts.

## Fears
Being ordered into war before logistics are ready.

## Ambitions
Rebuild army prestige against air/SS dominance.

## History
Commanded armored forces in Eastern occupation security operations.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- No protagonist connection.

## Current Situation
Preparing contingency plans for Turkish frontier scenarios.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could become anti-war without becoming anti-regime.
