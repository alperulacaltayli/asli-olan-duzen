# Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu

**Status:** Established Canon / Expanded Index

The sovereign state in which the novel currently takes place. Its current supreme ruler is [[Selim Arkan]], titled [[Ulu Hanlık Makamı|Ulu Han]].

## Current Situation
In 1985 the empire is technologically advanced, internationally powerful, and entering a period of heightened rivalry with the [[German Reich]]. It is not morally spotless: old executions and human-rights violations are expected to surface later.
