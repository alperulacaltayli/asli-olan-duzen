# Ulu Hanlık Makamı

**Status:** Proposed Organization  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Type:** Supreme sovereign office  
**Headquarters:** Kutlu Saray, Ankara  
**Leader:** [[Selim Arkan]]  
**Second-in-command:** [[Kaan Demirsoy]]  
**Established:** Post-1938 imperial reorganization — exact year [BELİRLENMESİ GEREKİYOR]  
**Purpose:** Central sovereign authority of the empire.

## Responsibilities
Final approval of strategic policy, high appointments, national addresses, war/peace decisions and imperial decrees.

## Internal Structure
Ulu Han, private council, chancellery gatekeepers, security detail, ceremonial office.

## Chain of Command
[[Selim Arkan]] → [[Kaan Demirsoy]] → ministries/high councils for implementation.

## Major Personnel
- [[Selim Arkan]]
- [[Kaan Demirsoy]]
- [[Sabiha Erten]]

## Political Influence
Highest legitimacy source; balances military, ministries and ideology.

## Military Influence
Supreme command authority when acting through [[Turkish Imperial High Command]].

## Resources
Imperial archives, decree authority, palace guard, crisis council access.

## Rival Institutions
- [[Ministry of Interior and Order]]
- [[Turkish Imperial High Command]]

## Allies
- [[Imperial Chancellery]]
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs]]

## Current Situation
Maintaining public calm while reviewing German intent.

## Historical Background
Evolved from post-Atatürk alternate leadership structure into imperial sovereign office.

## Public Structure
Publicly described as a lawful state institution serving the national order.

## Internal Structure
Internally divided into formal offices, personal networks, and senior advisers.

## Secret Structure
Status: Proposed. The office maintains classified continuity files on pre-Selim executions and human-rights violations.
