# Gökhan Aydemir

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Gök Kuvvetleri Başbuğu  
**Common Name:** Hava Mareşali Gökhan Aydemir  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Gök Kartal  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Imperial Air Force]]  
**Position:** Commander of the Imperial Air Force  
**Rank:** Hava Mareşali  
**Current Assignment:** Air superiority, strategic aviation, pilot training, TA-85 readiness  
**Primary Location:** Akıncı Gök Karargâhı, Ankara outskirts  
**Date of Birth:** 1928  
**Age:** 57 in 1985  
**Origin:** Eskişehir

## Role
Controls air force doctrine, readiness, strategic aviation, and pilot pipeline.

## Formal Authority
Commands all regular air force formations and air bases.

## Practical Influence
Can decide which technologies the air force takes seriously.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** air exercises, squadron transfers, pilot evaluations, technical requirement reviews
- **Cannot order:** armament production quotas or intelligence arrests
- **Can be ignored by:** industrial ministries can delay procurement; Ulu Han can redirect priorities
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]] on recommendation of [[Turan Erbay]]
- **Protected by:** air force prestige and TA-85 program success
- **Limited by:** industrial supply, fuel, trained pilot availability

## Reports To
[[Turan Erbay]] and ultimately [[Selim Arkan]]

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Baran İleri]]
- Regional air base commanders

## Political Relationships
- Needs [[Cevdet Bayraktar]] for aircraft production.
- Competes with strategic weapons advocates for budget.

## Military Relationships
- Works under [[Turan Erbay]].
- Coordinates with [[Cengiz Atalay]] through technical channels, not family channels.

## Personality
Elegant, exacting, proud of aviators.

## Leadership Style
Rewards competence, punishes careless bravado.

## Ideology / Beliefs
The empire’s future is decided in the sky and above the atmosphere.

## Personal Motivation
Prevent German aviation from regaining psychological dominance.

## Strengths
Technical literacy; pilot morale.

## Weaknesses
May overvalue aviation solutions to political problems.

## Fears
Hidden German detection or counter-stealth advantage.

## Ambitions
Create a fully independent Turkish air doctrine.

## History
Former test pilot turned commander after the early Gökkanat trials.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Potential future institutional link to [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]] if he enters military aviation.

## Current Situation
Reviewing why Horten and Gökkanat flew together over Istanbul.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could sponsor Oğuz’s LIDAR concept as a low-level experimental branch.
