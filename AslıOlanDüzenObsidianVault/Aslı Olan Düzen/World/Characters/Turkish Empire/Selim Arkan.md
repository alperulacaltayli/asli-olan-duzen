# Selim Arkan

**Status:** Established Canon / Expanded Profile  
**Official Title:** Ulu Han of the Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Common Name:** Selim Arkan  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Ulu Han  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Ulu Hanlık Makamı]]  
**Position:** Supreme ruler and final political authority  
**Rank:** Supreme civil-military authority  
**Current Assignment:** Directing imperial policy during rising German tension  
**Primary Location:** Kutlu Saray, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1939  
**Age:** 46 in 1985  
**Origin:** Ankara

## Role
Selim Arkan is the supreme ruler. He sets strategic direction, arbitrates between ministries and high command, and publicly embodies imperial continuity after [[Alperen Keser]] and [[İsmail Karaslan]].

## Formal Authority
Final approval over war, peace, high appointments, imperial decrees, strategic weapons policy, and emergency security orders.

## Practical Influence
Strong leader with real authority, but he depends on ministries, generals, intelligence reports, and older power networks to execute decisions.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** mobilization, ministerial reshuffles, classified investigations, diplomatic directives, and military readiness changes
- **Cannot order:** every local administrative detail personally; cannot instantly override logistical limits, regional facts, or entrenched bureaucratic delay
- **Can be ignored by:** almost no one openly; powerful institutions can delay, reinterpret, or filter implementation
- **Can be removed by:** only extraordinary elite consensus, dynastic/legal crisis, or military-political collapse; not by normal officials
- **Protected by:** Imperial Guard, loyal high command, legitimacy of the Ulu Hanlık
- **Limited by:** need to preserve continuity, avoid panic, and balance military, industry, intelligence, and public myth

## Reports To
No living superior; symbolically reports to the state, history, and imperial law.

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Kaan Demirsoy]]
- [[Turan Erbay]]
- [[Safiye Kutadgu]]
- [[Alp Erden]]
- [[Mete Kutlu]]

## Political Relationships
- Depends on [[Kaan Demirsoy]] for administrative translation of orders.
- Respects [[Turan Erbay]] but does not allow the military to dictate diplomacy.
- Uses [[Safiye Kutadgu]] to keep German relations from collapsing too early.

## Military Relationships
- Supreme authority over [[Turkish Imperial High Command]].
- Can convene the [[Kutlu Güvenlik Meclisi]].

## Personality
Disciplined, controlled, sharper than opponents expect; not a theatrical ruler.

## Leadership Style
Centralized but not impulsive. He asks for competing assessments, then issues short decisive orders.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Believes the empire must remain powerful, technically modern, and socially disciplined; privately understands that old imperial abuses can become future liabilities.

## Personal Motivation
Preserve the empire through the German crisis without letting either military zeal or diplomatic weakness destroy it.

## Strengths
Strategic patience; legitimacy; ability to force ministries to cooperate.

## Weaknesses
Receives filtered information; public myth limits how openly he can admit past wrongs.

## Fears
A German-triggered crisis before the empire is ready; exposure of historical state crimes at the wrong moment.

## Ambitions
Move the empire from dependence on German-derived technology to independent strategic superiority.

## History
Rose through administrative and state-security circles rather than battlefield command, which makes some older soldiers underestimate him.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Referenced in Chapter 1 as Ulu Han addressing students.
- Indirectly affects [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]] through school ideology and military ambition.

## Current Situation
After the German delegation visit, he is managing outward calm while ordering internal review of German intentions.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** May become the ruler who knows the empire must reform morally while still preparing for confrontation.
