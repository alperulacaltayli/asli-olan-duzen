# Baran İleri

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Gök Sistemleri Dairesi Başkanı  
**Common Name:** Baran İleri  
**Honorific / Epithet:** None  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Science and Strategic Research Council]]  
**Position:** Director of atmospheric and aerospace systems  
**Rank:** Senior technical director  
**Current Assignment:** Sensor research, atmospheric data, military-civilian technology bridge  
**Primary Location:** Eskişehir Gök Sistemleri Yerleşkesi  
**Date of Birth:** 1941  
**Age:** 44 in 1985  
**Origin:** Eskişehir

## Role
Technical administrator between researchers, air force, and industry.

## Formal Authority
Approves technical evaluation pathways for aerospace sensor projects.

## Practical Influence
Can decide whether a small prototype reaches senior eyes.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** technical reviews, laboratory access, field test scheduling
- **Cannot order:** procurement, military adoption, or classification level alone
- **Can be ignored by:** ministers and generals can overrule technical caution
- **Can be removed by:** [[Rana Tözün]] with ministerial approval
- **Protected by:** technical expertise and air force trust
- **Limited by:** budget and classification

## Reports To
[[Rana Tözün]]; coordinates with [[Gökhan Aydemir]]

## Direct Subordinates
- Laboratory chiefs
- Field test engineers

## Political Relationships
- Avoids ministry rivalry; quietly dislikes show projects.

## Military Relationships
- Technical liaison to [[Imperial Air Force]].

## Personality
Curious, skeptical, patient with machines more than people.

## Leadership Style
Asks “what failed?” before “who failed?”

## Ideology / Beliefs
Reality is measured, not declared.

## Personal Motivation
Find reliable detection methods for low-observable aircraft.

## Strengths
Technical judgment.

## Weaknesses
Bad at political defense of his projects.

## Fears
A rushed sensor system causing battlefield overconfidence.

## Ambitions
Build a sensor school independent of German methods.

## History
Worked on atmospheric instruments after the space program.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Potential future evaluator of [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]]’s LIDAR prototype.

## Current Situation
Reviewing atmospheric anomaly detection proposals.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could be the first official to say Oğuz’s idea is crude but promising.
