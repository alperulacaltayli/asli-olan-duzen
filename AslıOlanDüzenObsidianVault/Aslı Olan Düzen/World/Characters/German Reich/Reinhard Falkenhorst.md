# Reinhard Falkenhorst

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Führer und Reichskanzler des Großdeutschen Reiches  
**Common Name:** Führer Falkenhorst  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Der Erhalter  
**State:** German Reich  
**Institution:** [[Reich Chancellery]]  
**Position:** Supreme political leader  
**Rank:** Supreme party-state authority  
**Current Assignment:** Maintain Reich dominance amid Turkish technological rise  
**Primary Location:** Neue Reichskanzlei, Germania  
**Date of Birth:** 1922  
**Age:** 63 in 1985  
**Origin:** Hamburg

## Role
Supreme leader of the Nazi-controlled German state in 1985. He inherits Hitlerian structures but governs a mature, bureaucratized empire facing Turkish parity.

## Formal Authority
Final authority over party, state, war policy, senior appointments, and ideological direction.

## Practical Influence
Powerful but dependent on competing SS, military, industrial, and colonial networks.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** strategic directives, purges, appointments, military planning priorities
- **Cannot order:** instant industrial miracles, unquestioned loyalty from rival barons, or perfect intelligence
- **Can be ignored by:** no one openly; SS, military and industry can delay or distort implementation
- **Can be removed by:** succession coup, party-security alliance, catastrophic defeat; no legal ordinary removal
- **Protected by:** Führer Guard, party myth, SS security, patronage networks
- **Limited by:** succession anxiety, Turkish rise, industrial constraints

## Reports To
No formal superior; claims mandate of Reich and racial ideology.

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Martin Kessler]]
- [[Friedrich Wulfhart]]
- [[Karl-Heinz Adler]]
- [[Ulrich von Havel]]

## Political Relationships
- Keeps [[Martin Kessler]] close but distrusts his ambition.
- Balances [[Friedrich Wulfhart]] against [[Karl-Heinz Adler]].

## Military Relationships
- Supreme over [[Oberkommando der Wehrmacht]], but operational details filtered by [[Karl-Heinz Adler]].

## Personality
Coldly charismatic, aging but mentally sharp, obsessed with decline.

## Leadership Style
Divide-and-rule; gives overlapping mandates to prevent subordinates becoming indispensable.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Hard Nazi imperial doctrine adapted to a world where Turks are dangerous equals, not subordinates.

## Personal Motivation
Prevent the Reich from becoming the second power in a two-bloc world.

## Strengths
Institutional manipulation, ideological authority.

## Weaknesses
Paranoia; encourages rivalries that reduce efficiency.

## Fears
German youth seeing Turkish modernity as superior.

## Ambitions
Recover technological prestige and force Turkey back into dependency.

## History
Second-generation Nazi leader elevated after succession struggles of the 1960s.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- No direct connection to protagonist; his policies shape the world around [[Oğuz Kağan Atalay]].

## Current Situation
Interpreting Istanbul visit results and deciding whether Turkey is still manageable.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could drive escalation while believing he is preventing decline.
