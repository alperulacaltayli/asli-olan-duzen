# Turkish Imperial Titles and Epithets

**Status:** Proposed Terminology / Requires Author Approval  
**Scope:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu high offices and honorifics.

## Established Title
- **[[Selim Arkan|Ulu Han]]** — established canon. Supreme ruler; not merely a military rank.

## Proposed Offices
- **Başbuyrukçu** — chief administrator who turns the Ulu Han's will into binding orders. Used by [[Kaan Demirsoy]].
- **Ordu Başbuğu** — unified general staff chief. Used by [[Turan Erbay]].
- **Gök Kuvvetleri Başbuğu** — air force commander. Used by [[Gökhan Aydemir]].
- **Kara Kuvvetleri Başbuğu** — army commander. Used by [[Börü Alkan]].
- **Deniz Kuvvetleri Başbuğu** — navy commander. Used by [[Deniz Koray]].
- **İç Düzen Nazırı** — interior/order minister. Used by [[Alp Erden]].
- **Maarif ve Gençlik Nazırı** — education and youth minister, including empire-wide [[Kanat]] oversight. Used by [[Ziya Arat]].
- **Afrika Umumi Valisi** — governor-general for African imperial territories. Used by [[Haluk Sancar]].

## Proposed Honorifics / Epithets
These are not formal offices unless explicitly paired with a position.

- **Demir Başbuğ** — bestowed martial epithet for a hard, defensive chief of staff.
- **Gök Kartal** — aviation prestige epithet for an air commander.
- **Söz Elçisi** — diplomatic epithet for an exceptionally trusted foreign minister.
- **Bilge Hatun** — scholarly honorific for a senior woman of science or counsel.
- **Sessiz Börü** — informal intelligence epithet; not public-facing.
- **Güney Valisi** — common press shorthand for the African governor-general.

## Notes
These titles are intentionally administrative/military rather than fantasy-styled. They should feel like institutions created by a modernized imperial state, not medieval court ornaments.
