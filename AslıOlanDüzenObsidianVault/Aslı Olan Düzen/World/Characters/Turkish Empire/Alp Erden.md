# Alp Erden

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** İç Düzen Nazırı  
**Common Name:** Alp Erden  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Düzen Nazırı  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Ministry of Interior and Order]]  
**Position:** Minister of Interior and Order  
**Rank:** Senior minister  
**Current Assignment:** Internal administration, police, provincial governors, public order  
**Primary Location:** İç Düzen Nezareti, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1933  
**Age:** 52 in 1985  
**Origin:** Konya

## Role
Oversees internal order, governorship coordination, police directives, and public stability.

## Formal Authority
Commands civilian internal administration and domestic order organs.

## Practical Influence
Can shape how much dissent, scandal, or historical truth reaches public institutions.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** governor instructions, police priority, internal investigations
- **Cannot order:** military operations or foreign espionage beyond domestic boundaries
- **Can be ignored by:** intelligence chiefs can bypass him; strong governors may delay
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** provincial bureaucracy
- **Limited by:** chancellery wording, intelligence secrecy, military jurisdiction

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]] through [[Kaan Demirsoy]]

## Direct Subordinates
- [[Selahattin Akgün]]
- Regional governors

## Political Relationships
- Rivalry with [[Mete Kutlu]] over domestic intelligence.
- Needs [[Ziya Arat]] when school unrest touches youth policy.

## Military Relationships
- Coordinates with [[Börü Alkan]] only during emergency domestic support.

## Personality
Orderly, severe, suspicious.

## Leadership Style
Centralizes reports and prefers preventive action.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Order before confession; he fears truth released without control.

## Personal Motivation
Prevent old imperial abuses from becoming a destabilizing weapon.

## Strengths
Bureaucratic reach.

## Weaknesses
Instinct to suppress can worsen scandals.

## Fears
Human-rights revelations triggering youth disillusionment.

## Ambitions
Create an interior system no foreign intelligence can exploit.

## History
Built career cleaning up provincial disorder and hiding as much as solving.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Could indirectly affect school Kanat policy nationwide.

## Current Situation
Monitoring public reaction to German visit and Africa reform messaging.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** May oppose exposing past executions even when Selim wants controlled reform.
