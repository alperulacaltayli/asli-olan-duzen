# Ziya Arat

**Status:** Proposed Character  
**Official Title:** Maarif ve Gençlik Nazırı  
**Common Name:** Ziya Arat  
**Honorific / Epithet:** Terbiye Nazırı  
**State:** Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu  
**Institution:** [[Ministry of Education and Youth Order]]  
**Position:** Minister of education and youth formation  
**Rank:** Minister  
**Current Assignment:** Schools, curriculum, empire-wide Kanat system  
**Primary Location:** Maarif Nezareti, Ankara  
**Date of Birth:** 1934  
**Age:** 51 in 1985  
**Origin:** Samsun

## Role
Oversees education, youth discipline, curriculum, and the empire-wide [[Kanat]] system.

## Formal Authority
Controls national school directives and Kanat regulations.

## Practical Influence
Can shape a generation’s understanding of empire, duty, and history.

## Power Limits
- **Can order:** curriculum changes, Kanat audits, school inspections
- **Cannot order:** student arrests without interior/security channels
- **Can be ignored by:** elite schools can interpret rules creatively; local administrators delay
- **Can be removed by:** [[Selim Arkan]]
- **Protected by:** ideological importance of education
- **Limited by:** teacher culture and local school realities

## Reports To
[[Selim Arkan]]

## Direct Subordinates
- National Kanat Council
- Provincial education directors

## Political Relationships
- Works with [[Alp Erden]] when youth unrest becomes security issue.
- May clash with [[Rana Tözün]] over open inquiry.

## Military Relationships
- Military academies consult him but are not fully under him.

## Personality
Polished, paternal, image-conscious.

## Leadership Style
Prefers broad doctrine and symbolic campaigns.

## Ideology / Beliefs
Youth must be disciplined but not intellectually dead.

## Personal Motivation
Make Kanat system a source of national cohesion, not petty tyranny.

## Strengths
Public language; institutional reach.

## Weaknesses
May ignore school-level manipulation until scandal.

## Fears
Kanat system revealed as abusive or politically corrupted.

## Ambitions
Standardize Kanat across the empire.

## History
Former head of youth education reform.

## Relationship to Other Characters
- Directly relevant to [[B-3 Sınıf Profili]] and school Kanat politics.

## Current Situation
Preparing new year guidance after Selim Arkan’s student address.

## Potential Future Development
**Potential Future Connection — NOT CANON:** Could inspect Oğuz’s school if Alper/Tomris rivalry escalates.
