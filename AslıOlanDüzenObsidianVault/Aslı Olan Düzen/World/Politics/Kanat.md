# Kanat

**Status:** Established Canon / Expanded System Note

Empire-wide student responsibility and rank system. Author decision: the Kanat system exists across the entire [[Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu]], not only in Oğuz's school.

## Known Levels
- One Kanat: entry/basic level.
- Two Kanat: proven respectful and helpful student.
- Three Kanat: group leadership and responsibility.
- Four Kanat: wider organization of Three Kanats and school operations.
- Five Kanat: school president's deputy / high student office.
- Six Kanat: school president / highest student office.
