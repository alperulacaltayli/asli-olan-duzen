# World Powers - Strategic Overview

**Status:** Proposed Macro-Structure / Requires Author Approval  
**Current Year:** 1985

## Major Power Centers
- [[Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu]] — led by [[Selim Arkan]], technologically advanced, disciplined, morally complicated, rising beyond German dependency.
- [[German Reich]] — led by [[Reinhard Falkenhorst]], Nazi-controlled, authoritarian, internally rivalrous, threatened by Turkish parity.
- Other major states: **[BELİRLENMESİ GEREKİYOR]** — the manuscript mentions America and Soviets historically, but current status remains unconfirmed.

## Turkish-German Relationship
**Public:** friendship, diplomacy, technical exchange.  
**Internal:** mutual suspicion, aviation rivalry, intelligence collection, ideological contempt.  
**Secret:** controlled clandestine channels, including the author-confirmed Nazi connection of [[Cengiz Atalay]].

## Background World Message
Africa remains a background moral/political mirror. Turkish reform messaging contrasts with Nazi brutality, but the Büyük Türk İmparatorluğu also carries older executions and human-rights violations that can later surface.

## Strategic Competition Axes
- Aviation: [[Imperial Air Force]] vs [[Luftwaffe High Command]]
- Sensors: [[Science and Strategic Research Council]] vs [[Reich Scientific and Special Technology Office]]
- Intelligence: [[Imperial Intelligence Directorate]] vs [[Reich Foreign Intelligence Service]] / [[SS-Reichssicherheitsapparat]]
- Diplomacy: [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs]] vs [[Reich Foreign Office]]
- Africa: [[Haluk Sancar]] reform line vs [[Bruno Epp]] colonial exploitation line
